Why this authentication layer?

SecureGraphical combines conventional password authentication with a dynamic graphical password mechanism to provide an additional authentication layer.

🎨
8 Colour Sectors The authentication wheel contains eight independently identifiable colour sectors.
🔤
16 Character Alphabet Eight lowercase characters and eight numerical characters are distributed dynamically.
🔄
Dynamic Rotation Characters can be rotated clockwise or anti-clockwise before selection.
👁️
Shoulder-Surfing Resistance A changing character arrangement makes direct observation less useful than observing a conventional password.
🧩
Multi-Layer Authentication Text password and graphical authentication are required before dashboard access.
